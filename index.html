A real-time price index for India’s airfares.

Airfares entered India’s Consumer Price Index with the 2024 series, yet nothing tracks them automatically. Alavia collects fares from airline and travel-portal websites, cleans them, and turns them into a route-weighted index economists can plug straight into the CPI.

From a fare on a website to a number in the CPI.

Four stages, each one automated. The path below runs the way the data does.

  1. SRC Airline websites and travel aggregators Plus web crawlers and simulation data during testing
  2. 1

    Collect

    Scrapers and official APIs capture the fare, taxes, travel date and booking time, sampled at the CPI’s advance-purchase windows: 21 days out for domestic, 60 for international.

    Playwright, Scrapy, BeautifulSoup, Airflow

  3. 2

    Clean and validate

    Duplicates are removed, missing values handled, routes and airline names normalised, and abnormal fares flagged before they can reach the index.

    Pandas, NumPy, Scikit-learn

  4. 3

    Compute the index

    Route-level indices are calculated against the 2024 base with Laspeyres and chain-weighted methods, then aggregated into one all-India figure.

    Python, FastAPI, PostgreSQL, Redis

  5. 4

    Publish

    A dashboard for economists with interactive charts and CPI augmentation reports, and a secured REST API for MoSPI’s CPI pipeline.

    React, Recharts, REST API gateway

  6. CPI MoSPI’s Consumer Price Index Secure data transfer through an economist interface

Commercial fare data only. No personal data is collected, in line with the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023. Scrapers respect robots.txt and each site’s terms, and official APIs are preferred wherever they exist.

The Alavia Airfare Price Index

Base year 2024 = 100, the same base as the CPI’s new series. Computed live in your browser from 2,100 fare records across 30 routes, 5 airlines and both directions of travel.

How it’s calculated

Each route gets an elementary index: the geometric mean of every airline-and-direction fare relative to its 2024 level.

Ir,t = 100 × ( ∏ pl,t ⁄ pl,2024 )1/n

Route indices are then combined with fixed base-period weights, Laspeyres style, into the all-India index.

It = Σ wr · Ir,t

The prototype weights all 30 routes equally. In production, wr comes from DGCA route-wise passenger traffic, and weights are chain-linked each year.

    Who benefits.

    Government and MoSPI

    Faster policy decisions
    Timely, data-driven evidence for fare and aviation policy.
    Inflation monitoring
    Tracks airfare inflation and catches sudden price spikes.
    Lower collection cost
    Automated collection cuts the time and money spent on manual surveys.
    Economic planning
    Supports planning for transport, tourism and inflation targets.

    Economists and analysts

    Real-time updates
    Frequently refreshed fares instead of periodic snapshots.
    Historical comparison
    Compare airfare trends across any period on a consistent base.
    Route-wise analysis
    Compare prices across routes and airlines, not just a national average.

    Travellers

    Market transparency
    One view of fare trends across airlines and travel portals.
    Consumer awareness
    Unusual fare increases are flagged in public, not buried in a survey.

    Alavia is an automated, real-time airfare price index for India. It gathers fares from airline and travel-portal websites, cleans and validates them, and turns them into a route-weighted index — published live through a dashboard and an API, ready to strengthen the Consumer Price Index. Everything on this page, from the fare board to the route charts to the data table, runs from that same index, computed live in your browser.
